Qoraflow ("Qoraflow," "we," "us" or "our") builds AI voice agents that answer and make phone calls for businesses, along with the Qoraflow app that lets those businesses track their calls. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you visit qoraflow.com, contact us, start a free trial or use our services.

01Who this policy covers

  • Website visitors: people who browse qoraflow.com or fill in our forms.
  • Customers: businesses that sign up for a free trial or a paid plan, and the people who use the Qoraflow app on their behalf.
  • Callers and contacts: people who call a business that uses Qoraflow, or who are called by a Qoraflow agent on that business's behalf.

When we handle information about callers and contacts, we do so on behalf of our customer, the business you called or that called you. That business decides how the information is used. If you have questions about a specific call, please contact that business directly. We will also help where we can.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details such as your full name, email address and phone number.
  • Business details such as your business name, a description of your business, hours, services, prices and FAQs you share so we can set up your agent.
  • Account and billing details. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Anything you send us in emails, forms or support requests.

Call information

  • Phone numbers, caller ID and, where provided, caller names.
  • Call recordings, transcripts and AI generated call summaries.
  • Appointment details, messages and requests made during a call.
  • Call details such as date, time, duration and outcome.
  • Caller history used for our "remembers every caller" feature, so a returning caller can be recognized.

Lead information

For customers on plans that include outbound calling, we may process information that people submit through our customers' advertisements, landing pages or lead forms, including their request and consent to be contacted.

Website voice demo

When you use "Talk to Qoraflow" on our website, we ask for your name and phone number so our team can follow up with you, and your browser sends your microphone audio to our voice provider, Retell AI, and the speech and language model providers it uses, so our AI agent can hear you and reply. The call, its transcript and basic technical details (such as the page you started it from and the time) may be processed and kept to run and improve the demo. The call only starts when you tap to talk, ends when you hang up or leave the page, and is limited to 4 minutes. Please don't share sensitive personal information during a demo call.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information, IP address, approximate location and pages you visit.
  • Information from cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).

The "Talk to Qoraflow" feature on our website uses your microphone only after you turn it on, and only to animate the visual on the page. That audio is processed in your browser; it is not recorded or sent to us.

03How we use information

  • To provide our services: answering and making calls, booking appointments, routing calls, taking messages and recognizing returning callers.
  • To run the Qoraflow app, including recordings, transcripts, summaries and analytics.
  • To set up free trials and contact you about your request.
  • To process payments and manage accounts.
  • To provide support and send service updates.
  • To keep our services safe, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve the quality and accuracy of our services.
  • To send marketing emails, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

Our voice agents use artificial intelligence, provided in part by trusted technology partners, to understand callers and respond.

04Call recording and consent

Calls handled by Qoraflow agents may be recorded and transcribed so businesses can review them in the Qoraflow app. Agents can be set up to tell callers that the call may be recorded and that they are speaking with an AI assistant.

Our customers are responsible for giving any notices and getting any consents required by law for recording calls and for contacting people, including in states that require every party on a call to agree to recording.

05How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described here:

  • With the business you called or that called you: call recordings, transcripts and details go to the Qoraflow customer the call was for.
  • With service providers: companies that help us run our services, such as phone carriers, cloud hosting, AI processing, payments, email and analytics. They may only use the information to provide services to us.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of Qoraflow, our customers or others.
  • In a business transfer: if Qoraflow is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.
  • With your permission: in any other case where you ask us to or agree.

06Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep our website working, remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site and measure our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the website works.

07Data retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Call recordings and transcripts are kept for [90 days] by default unless the customer chooses a different period. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed, unless we must keep it to meet legal, tax or accounting requirements or to resolve disputes.

08Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us right away.

09Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete: you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it.
  • Marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Calls: you can ask not to be called again by telling the agent during a call or by contacting the business or us.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live, such as California, you may have additional rights. We will not treat you differently for using them.

To make a request, email us at hello@qoraflow.com. We may need to verify your identity first. If your request is about a call made for one of our customers, we may pass it on to that business.

10Children's privacy

Our services are meant for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will let customers know by email or in the Qoraflow app.
