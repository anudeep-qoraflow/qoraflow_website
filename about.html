About Qoraflow

We're building the front desk every business deserves.

Qoraflow gives small and growing businesses an AI receptionist that answers every call, books every appointment and remembers every customer, at a price that finally makes sense.

Why we exist

The phone is still where business is won or lost.

Think about the dentist with a waiting room full of patients, the plumber under a sink, the salon owner mid-appointment. Their phone rings, and they can't answer. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list.

Big companies solve this with full reception teams and call centers. Small businesses are left choosing between an expensive hire, a voicemail box nobody checks, or simply losing customers.

We started Qoraflow to end that trade-off. Modern AI can now hold a natural, helpful phone conversation, and we believe that power belongs to the corner shop just as much as the corporation.

Our mission

Make a world-class front desk affordable for every business, so no customer ever goes unanswered.

What we believe

Four promises behind everything we build.
