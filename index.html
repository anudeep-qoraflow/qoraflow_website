FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What is a Qoraflow voice agent?

An AI receptionist that answers your business calls, books appointments, answers common questions, routes urgent calls and takes messages, 24/7.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

Our agents sound natural and friendly. You decide how your agent introduces itself, and whether it mentions it's an AI.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. Forward your current number to your Qoraflow agent, or we can set you up with a new one.

How long does setup take?

One day. Send us your hours, services and FAQs, and your agent will be trained, tested and taking calls within 24 hours.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Your agent books straight into the calendar you already use. Tell us which one and we'll connect it during setup.

How does it remember returning callers?

When someone calls from a number they have used before, your agent recognizes them and can greet them by name with their past calls in mind.

What do I see in the Qoraflow app?

Every call with its recording, transcript and summary, plus bookings, messages and analytics, on web and mobile.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month and you can cancel anytime.
