This Refund Policy explains how payments, cancellation and refunds work for Qoraflow plans. It is part of our Terms of Service. If anything here differs from the Terms, the Terms apply.

01The short version

  • The free trial costs nothing and never turns into a paid plan on its own.
  • Plans are month to month and billed in advance.
  • You can cancel at any time. Your service continues until the end of the month you have paid for.
  • Fees already paid are not refunded, including for partial months, except where the law requires a refund.

02Free 14 day trial

Eligible businesses may get a free trial, in which we set up a live voice agent at no cost for 14 days. Trials are limited to one per business. We do not charge you during the trial, and the trial does not turn into a paid plan automatically: at the end of the trial, you can choose a plan or your agent will be switched off. Because nothing is charged, there is nothing to refund.

03Monthly plans and billing

Plans are billed monthly, in advance, to the payment method on file. Each payment covers the month ahead.

04Cancelling your plan

Plans are month to month, with no long term contract. You can cancel at any time, and cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Your voice agent keeps working until then, and you will not be charged again.

To cancel, email us at contact@qoraflow.com from the email address on your account.

05Refunds

Fees already paid are not refundable, including for partial months, except where required by law. Cancelling part way through a billing period stops future charges but does not refund the current period.

06Price changes and failed payments

Price changes: we will give you at least 30 days' notice before changing the price of your plan, so you can cancel before the new price applies.

Failed payments: if a payment fails, we may suspend the Services until the balance is paid.
